# Publicité vidéo BarPilote — Prompt IA

Pour obtenir un meilleur résultat, générer la publicité en plusieurs scènes de 5 à 8 secondes, puis les assembler dans CapCut. Utiliser de préférence les prompts en anglais, car les générateurs vidéo les interprètent généralement mieux.

## Prompt principal

```text
Create a premium cinematic commercial for “BarPilote”, a modern SaaS platform for bars and restaurants in Kinshasa.

The story takes place inside a stylish, lively African bar in Kinshasa at night. A group of three young Congolese friends, two men and one woman, sit together at table number 4. The atmosphere is warm and elegant, with dark green decor, subtle orange lighting, realistic customers in the background and professional bartenders.

The friends scan the QR code placed on their table using their smartphones. Each person browses a modern restaurant menu and adds a different drink to the same shared order: one soda, one beer and one cocktail. Their expressions are natural, relaxed and impressed.

Show the waiter instantly receiving one consolidated order on his smartphone, accepting it and preparing the service. The waiter brings the drinks to table 4. The three friends smile and enjoy the fast experience.

End with a clean premium shot of a smartphone, the BarPilote logo, and the message:
“BarPilote — Commandez ensemble. Gérez simplement.”

Premium technology commercial, authentic Congolese cast, natural acting, realistic hands, realistic smartphones, cinematic camera movement, shallow depth of field, warm lighting, dark emerald green and orange brand colors, professional color grading, high-end commercial photography, realistic motion, 16:9 format, 4K advertising style.
```

## Prompts par scène

### Scène 1 — Arrivée dans le bar

```text
Wide cinematic establishing shot of a stylish modern bar in Kinshasa at night. Three young Congolese friends, two men and one woman, enter the bar and happily sit together at table number 4. Premium dark emerald green interior with warm orange lighting, realistic customers, elegant and welcoming atmosphere. Smooth forward camera movement, natural acting, high-end African hospitality commercial, 16:9.
```

### Scène 2 — Scan du QR code

```text
Cinematic close-up of three young Congolese friends sitting around table number 4. Each person uses a modern smartphone to scan the same QR code placed at the center of the table. Realistic hands and phones, natural smiles, warm bar lighting, shallow depth of field, smooth camera movement, premium technology advertisement, 16:9.
```

### Scène 3 — Commande partagée

```text
Over-the-shoulder cinematic shots of three friends ordering drinks from their smartphones at the same bar table. The first selects a soda, the second selects a beer, and the third selects a cocktail. Their actions contribute to one shared table order. Natural reactions, realistic smartphone interaction, elegant dark green and orange atmosphere, premium SaaS commercial, 16:9.
```

### Scène 4 — Réception par le serveur

```text
A professional young Congolese waiter inside a modern Kinshasa bar receives a new consolidated table order on his smartphone. He looks at the notification, smiles confidently and taps to accept the order. Smooth cinematic close-up, dark emerald uniform with a subtle orange accent, realistic motion, warm lighting, premium hospitality technology commercial, 16:9.
```

### Scène 5 — Livraison

```text
The same Congolese waiter arrives at table number 4 carrying a soda, a cold beer and a colorful cocktail. He places each drink in front of the three friends. They smile, impressed by the fast service. Cinematic slow motion, natural expressions, warm elegant lighting, premium bar commercial, 16:9.
```

### Scène finale — BarPilote

```text
Premium closing shot for a technology advertisement. A modern smartphone rests on table number 4 beside three drinks in a stylish bar. Dark emerald green background with a subtle orange glow. The camera slowly moves closer to the phone. Clean space in the center for adding the BarPilote logo and the slogan during editing. Cinematic lighting, elegant composition, luxury SaaS advertising style, 16:9.
```

## Voix off

```text
Au bar, chacun choisit ce qu’il aime. Avec BarPilote, vos clients scannent la table, commandent ensemble et suivent une facture commune. Le serveur reçoit une seule commande, claire et instantanée. Moins d’attente, moins d’erreurs, plus de satisfaction. BarPilote : commandez ensemble, gérez simplement.
```

## Recommandation de montage

Ne pas demander à l’IA d’écrire précisément l’interface ou le logo dans la vidéo : elle risque de les déformer. Ajouter ensuite dans CapCut le véritable enregistrement de BarPilote sur les écrans des téléphones, ainsi que le logo et le slogan.
